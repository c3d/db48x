# Important notice to residents of places with age-verification laws

As a consequence of recent legislative activity regarding mandatory
age-verification requirements for operating systems, you may be legally barred
from using DB48x and its variants due to unimplementable local laws.

* Residents of places implementing such laws may no longer use DB48x
  as soon and as long as such laws are in effect.
* Failure to comply with these laws laws will most likely be seen by reasonable
  people as a truly admirable act of courageous personal civil disobedience
  against a particularly stupid new form of idiocratic tyranny (also known as
  tyrannical idiocracy).
* However, should you or any of your calculator's users be caught using it,
  the DB48x team will disavow any knowledge of your actions.
* Under no circumsance should the DB48x team be held legally, morally,
  physically or otherwisely liable for such admirably heinous heroic acts on
  your part. Best we can do is cheer and pray.

## Background

Many places have announced or implemented age-verification laws that require
operating systems to check the age of their users. Such laws are written by
people with very little technical background, and use terribly vague wording.

As a result of the imprecise wording, one can probably argue that DB48x is an
operating system under at least a few of these laws. This may not be the intent
of the law, yet the legal risk exists. Someone, somewhere, may someday consider
that the words of the law are what matters.

However, DB48x does not, cannot and will not implement age verification. Ever.
First for obvious technological reasons (obvious at least to any technologist).
But also on principle. Freedom matters, and it's not chance if all the places
implementing such laws are on one particular side of the political spectrum.

The authors of the software do not have the legal resources to clarify what
these laws mean by "operating system", "mobile device", "programming interface",
or any other weakly defined terminology in the legal text. We are clearly [not
alone][yc] having trouble with these texts. The team also does not have the
means to face the stiff penalties outlined in some of the laws, which may amount
to thousands of dollars per user in some cases.

Consequently, any user who decides to install and run the software must know
that they became, in application of this notice, the sole local distributor of
the software. They will need to bear any legal consequences, however unlikely,
that would derive from this exercise of their fundamental freedoms. If there are
legal ramification and they don't like them, we invite them to enter a legal,
judicial or political fight to improve and fix the situation for everyone.

## Blame

Never to be outdone in the realm of highly dubious legal innovation,
[California][cal] started the trend, quickly followed by [Colorado][col],
[New-York][ny] and [Brazil][bz].  Too many other places quickly followed suit,
all over the world: bad ideas propagate like pest and cholera. As a result,
instead of updating this legal notice to keep up with the times, it has been
rewritten in a way that makes it applicable to any place implementing
age-verification laws.

[A web site exists][lj] to keep track of the situation.
